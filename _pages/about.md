Hi! I’m Aditya, a 4th-year Computer Engineering student at the University of Waterloo. I’m broadly interested in building **sample-efficient autonomous agents** that learn efficiently and generalize beyond their training distribution. My work centers on the intersection of **reinforcement learning, representation learning, and generative modelling**, with a focus on improving generalization and long-horizon consistency by leveraging synthetic experience.

In past research projects, I’ve worked on **generative world models for humanoid control**, **controllable diffusion-based medical data generation**, and **flight operations modelling**, giving me a deep appreciation for how generative modelling can drive sample-efficiency and serve as a foundation for autonomous, self-improving agents.

More recently, I’ve explored applying RL techniques to small LLMs, including **meta-algorithms for inference optimization**, **preference modelling under limited capacity**, and iterative **self-play for post-training alignment**. I also bring a systems lens to efficiency through projects such as implementing a Diffusion Transformer block on FPGA hardware with custom neural kernels.

Looking forward, I’m excited to push the boundaries of **reasoning, generalization, and efficiency**, especially in **reinforcement learning** settings grounded in physical systems like **embodied AI**. I plan to pursue graduate studies starting in 2026 and am actively exploring labs working at this intersection. If you know of one, I’d love to connect!
